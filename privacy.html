1. What UP2? is

UP2? helps adults arrange short, spontaneous, in-person meet-ups. A person creates an event, others ask to join, the creator confirms who comes, and the group gets a temporary chatroom until the event ends.

2. What we collect

We do not collect payment details, contacts, or your photo library. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

3. Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in who can see an event can see the event details and its creator's name, photo and profile card.
  • The event creator can see the name, photo, age, gender, hometown, three points and message of anyone who asks to join.
  • Confirmed group members can see each other's profile cards and the chat.
  • Nobody can see your email, phone number, exact stored coordinates, or your stats.

4. How long we keep it

  • Events, chatrooms and messages are permanently deleted from our active Supabase servers about 2 hours after the event start time, or sooner if the creator cancels.
  • Join requests are deleted with the event they belong to.
  • Your profile (name, age, gender, selfie, points, stats) is kept until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account (via Settings) permanently removes your profile, live selfie, events, requests, messages, and login data from our live database immediately.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and login from Sydney, Australia.
  • Brevo sends login emails from the European Union (France).
  • OpenStreetMap-based map tiles show maps; the map provider sees your device's IP address when tiles load.
  • FlutterFlow / Google Flutter is used to build the app and does not receive your profile data.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your data in Settings. For compliance requests or data portability questions under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, GDPR, or US State laws, contact privacy@up2app.app. We reply to all compliance requests within 30 days.

7. Age Restrictions

UP2? is strictly for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we will terminate the account immediately.

8. Safety, Reports, and Blocking

Profiles are self-reported and we do not run background checks. Users can block or report anyone directly from their profile card. Reports are kept strictly confidential. Blocked users can no longer see each other's events or profiles.
