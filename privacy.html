3. Optional sensitive details

Cultural identity (shown as flags, for example) and sexuality are "sensitive information" under Australian privacy law. Adding them is entirely optional. If you add them, you consent to us storing them and showing them on your profile card and event cards to other signed-in users. Your cultural identity can also be used by others to filter events (for example, the Travellers filter on JOIN shows plans hosted by people who share a culture). You can change or remove these details at any time in Settings, and they are removed immediately.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the app: show events near you, let hosts confirm guests, open group chats and send notifications.
  • To apply the limits hosts set, such as age range and gender.
  • To keep people safe: investigate reports, stop banned or underage people from returning, and enforce our Terms.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of you for anyone else.

5. Who can see what

  • Signed-in users who can see an event see its details and the host's profile card: first name, selfie, age, and any optional details the host chose to add.
  • The host sees the profile card and message of everyone who asks to join.
  • The confirmed group sees each other's profile cards and the chat. In chat, names show as first name and last initial.
  • The exact meeting spot is shown only after the host locks in the chat, and only to the group. Before that, people see a rough area at most.
  • Nobody else can see your email address, exact stored coordinates, activity counts, or reports you made.
  • Blocked people can no longer see your profile or events, and you will not see theirs.

6. Location, camera and notifications

  • Location is used only while the app is open, to show nearby events and centre maps. We never track your location in the background. The meeting point you set as a host is stored so your group can find it.
  • Camera is used only to take your live selfie. We never record video or open your photo library.
  • Notifications (for example "You're in!", new UP2 ITs and chat messages) are sent only if you allow them. You can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, or by emailing privacy@up2app.app. When you do:

  • Your profile, selfies, optional details, join requests and sign-in are deleted straight away.
  • Messages you already sent stay in that chat, under your name, until the chat deletes itself as normal. This stops people deleting their account to hide harassment.
  • If you are hosting an event that is already underway, it continues for the group until it ends.
  • To protect other users, we keep your name, age, last selfie and sign-in email in a locked record for 30 days, so you can still be reported and, if needed, banned. Users can never see this record. It is then deleted, unless a report about you is still open.

9. Safety, reports and moderation

  • Profiles are self-reported. We do not check IDs or run background checks.
  • You can report or block anyone from their profile card, an event or the chat. When you report someone, we save a copy of the relevant event, chat messages and their selfie as evidence, even after the chat deletes itself.
  • If several different people report the same person, that account is automatically hidden until we review it. Accounts may also be held for review if their name matches a known problem.
  • Reports are confidential. We never tell someone who reported them.

11. Data stored outside Australia

Our main database is in Australia. Some providers above are located in the United States or the European Union, so limited information (such as your email address, device token, IP address or a map search) may be handled there. We only use providers with strong privacy and security commitments.

12. Authorities and the law

We may share information with police or other authorities if the law requires it, or if we reasonably believe it is needed to prevent serious harm to someone.

13. Security and data breaches

Information is encrypted in transit, and database rules limit each person to the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as the law requires.

14. Your rights and complaints

  • You can view and update most of your information in Settings, and delete your account yourself.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing privacy@up2app.app. We reply within 30 days. This includes requests under the GDPR or US state privacy laws.
  • If you are unhappy with how we handled a privacy matter, contact us first. If we cannot fix it, you can complain to the OAIC at oaic.gov.au.

15. Age limit

UP2? is strictly for people aged 18 or over. If someone enters an age under 18, they cannot create an account, and their sign-in email is blocked from trying again. If we learn an under-18 has an account, we delete it.

16. Changes and contact

If we make important changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top and let you know in the app. For anything privacy related, email privacy@up2app.app.
