1. What UP2? is

UP2? helps adults arrange short, spontaneous, in-person meet-ups. A person creates an event, others ask to join, the creator confirms who comes, and the group gets a temporary chatroom until the event ends.

3. Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in who can see an event can see the event details and its creator's name, photo and profile card.
  • The event creator can see the name, photo, age, gender, hometown, three points and message of anyone who asks to join.
  • Confirmed group members can see each other's profile cards and the chat.
  • Nobody can see your email, phone number, exact stored coordinates, or your stats.

4. How long we keep it

  • Events, chatrooms and messages are permanently deleted about 2 hours after the event start time, or sooner if the creator cancels.
  • Join requests are deleted with the event they belong to.
  • Your profile (name, age, gender, selfie, points, stats) is kept until you delete your account, so you stay signed in between events.
  • Delete account (Settings, bottom of the panel) removes your profile, your selfie, your events, your requests and your messages, and your login.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and login from Sydney, Australia.
  • Brevo sends login emails from the European Union (France).
  • OpenStreetMap-based map tiles show maps; the map provider sees your device's IP address when tiles load.
  • FlutterFlow / Google Flutter is used to build the app and does not receive your profile data.

We only share data with these providers to run the app, or where the law requires.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your three points and selfie in Settings, and delete your account and all data at any time in the app. For anything else (a copy of your data, a correction, a complaint) email privacy@up2app.app. Depending on where you live, you have specific rights under global privacy laws:

  • Australian Users: We handle your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).
  • European & UK Users: Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, object to processing, and exercise data portability.
  • US Users: We respect state-level consumer privacy rights, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), allowing you to request data deletion and opt out of data sales (note: we do not sell your data).

We reply to all compliance requests within 30 days.

7. Age

UP2? is for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

8. Safety and reports

Profiles are self-reported and we do not run background checks. If someone breaks the rules you can report them; we may remove accounts and keep the minimum data needed to stop repeat abuse.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is limited by database rules, so people only see data for events and groups they belong to. No system is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect. Continuing to use UP2? afterwards means you accept the updated policy.

11. Profile photos and age checks

  • Only your current photo is kept. Your selfie is valid for 2 weeks. After that you must take a new one in the app before you can create or join events. When you save the new selfie, your old photo and any unsaved retakes are deleted from our storage.
  • Camera only. Photos cannot be uploaded from your gallery, so the photo is a live picture of you.
  • Why we do this. It keeps profiles real and lets us re-check that people are who they say they are. If someone reports a profile as under 18 or fake, we compare the current photo with the profile age.
  • Under-18 attempts. If you enter an age under 18 at sign-up, we do not create a profile, and a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of your email or phone number is kept so that it cannot be used to sign up again. We do not keep the age you typed.
  • If a report is open against you, your current photo is kept until we finish the review, even if you replace it or delete your account, and is then deleted.

12. Reports and blocking

  • You can report or block anyone from their profile card. Reports are private and the reported person is not told who reported them.
  • A report keeps the reported person's name, age and photo at that time, your reason and details, and, if you were in the same chat, the last 50 messages of that chat (chats are otherwise deleted after the event).
  • Reports are read only by us. Dismissed reports are deleted after 90 days and actioned reports after 12 months.
  • Several different people reporting one profile can hide it from other users until we review it.
  • Blocking means you and that person no longer see each other's events.
